# Reading Notes

Reading notes are lightweight records grouped by one primary category. Use tags for cross-category attributes.

# Categories

- [Technology](technology/index.md) - engineering, AI, architecture, tools, technical papers, and implementation writeups.
- [Work](work/index.md) - management, collaboration, retrospectives, organizational process, and career material.
- [Product](product/index.md) - product design, UX, growth, requirements, and user research.
- [Business](business/index.md) - companies, industries, commercial models, markets, and strategy.
- [Personal](personal/index.md) - personal growth, learning systems, life systems, and non-work methods.
- [Misc](misc/index.md) - material that does not yet have a stable home.
